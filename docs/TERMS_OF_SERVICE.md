# Semo0o AI — Terms of Service

**Status: Draft — legal review required before public launch.**

## Service scope

Semo0o AI provides agent orchestration, workspace, analysis, memory, and model-provider features where the deployment has configured the relevant provider. A catalog entry does not guarantee that an integration is available.

## Responsible use

Customers are responsible for authorization of repositories, accounts, files, prompts, and actions submitted to the service. Dangerous actions such as code execution, file mutation, email, calendar, browser actions, and external commits require explicit permissions and may require approval.

## No unverified claims

The service must not claim that an external action occurred unless the deployment has provider evidence. Model output is not a substitute for professional, medical, legal, financial, or safety advice. BodyMap/anatomy content is informational and must not be used for diagnosis or emergency decisions.

## Accounts and organizations

Customers must protect credentials, use MFA where available, assign least-privilege roles, and promptly remove members or integrations that no longer need access. The organization owner is responsible for billing and authorized use.

## Availability and limits

Plans, quotas, rate limits, provider outages, sandbox limits, retention windows, and recovery objectives must be specified in the commercial order or deployment configuration. Local tests do not constitute an availability commitment.

## Termination and data

The deployment must document export, deletion, backup expiry, suspension, and termination behavior. Deletion requests may be delayed by legally required retention or backup expiration.

## Launch requirements

The operator must complete governing law, liability, acceptable-use, data-processing, pricing, refund, support, and contact sections with qualified legal review before accepting customers.
